One line per test angle: the angle and the brightness averaged over one whole pitch (ribbon and gap) at that slot. Values over 1 are read as 0–255 photo readings. Angles given on one side only are mirrored.

Calibration combs

Two identical combs, one slot per test angle from −90° to +90° at this pitch: hang a ribbon through each slot pair, light it as it will be shown, photograph it straight on from the viewing distance, and read each pitch's brightness.
